### Day 1: Welcome to Your 90-Day IELTS Journey! 🚀

> 💡 *Today is our first day. We will start building a rock-solid foundation, understand the test format, and establish daily habits.*

- [ ] **Task 1: Exam Overview:** Get a clear idea of the IELTS exam format (Reading, Listening, Writing, Speaking, scoring & bands 1–9) from official Cambridge resources or YouTube.
- [ ] **Task 2: Core Vocabulary:** Write and memorize 20 essential Academic Vocabulary words with sample sentences in your notebook.
- [ ] **Task 3: Ear Training:** Listen at least 2 times to an episode from BBC Learning English or 6 Minute English podcast without subtitles, then with transcript.
- [ ] **Task 4: Skim Reading:** Read a short editorial article from The Guardian or The Daily Star and highlight at least 5 unknown collocations.
- [ ] **Task 5: Typing Drill:** Practice typing an English passage for 30 minutes to prepare for computer-delivered IELTS and writing stamina.

---

### Day 2: Grammar & Reading Habit 📚

> 💡 *Building on day 1, today we expand our reading stamina and brush up on core English sentence structures.*

- [ ] **Task 1: Grammar Refresh:** Revise Parts of Speech and Basic Sentence Structure (Subject, Verb, Object, Adverbial clauses) from a grammar guide.
- [ ] **Task 2: Word Bank:** Look up meanings and connotations of yesterday's marked words, recording phonetic transcription and antonyms.
- [ ] **Task 3: Academic Vocab:** Learn 20 new Academic Word List (AWL) terms and construct compound sentences using each.
- [ ] **Task 4: Video Immersion:** Watch a 10-minute TED Talk on education or technology with English subtitles, noting natural transitions.
- [ ] **Task 5: Typing Stamina:** Transcribe a 200-word paragraph by sight without looking at the keyboard for 30 minutes.

---

### Day 3: Listening & Speaking Focus 🎧

> 💡 *Today we begin training the auditory processing channels and unlocking spoken fluency.*

- [ ] **Task 1: Section 1 Listening:** Listen to a Cambridge Section 1 audio (names, dates, phone numbers) to observe British and Australian pronunciations.
- [ ] **Task 2: Oral Inertia Drill:** Read 2 paragraphs aloud from an English newspaper with exaggerated clear enunciation to build tongue flexibility.
- [ ] **Task 3: Speaking Part 1:** Speak for 2 minutes on 'Describe your Hometown / Neighborhood', record on your phone, and analyze pauses.
- [ ] **Task 4: Topic Vocabulary:** Learn 15 vocabulary items related to Urban Life vs Countryside (e.g., bustling, tranquil, infrastructure, amenities).
- [ ] **Task 5: Typing Drill:** Type your recorded speaking thoughts into a Word doc for 25 minutes, noting grammar errors.

---

### Day 4: Reading Strategies (Skimming & Scanning) 📖

> 💡 *Master the two cardinal techniques of IELTS reading: high-speed skimming for main ideas and precision scanning for specifics.*

- [ ] **Task 1: Skimming Technique:** Practice skimming a 700-word National Geographic article in 3 minutes. Write a 2-sentence summary of the main argument.
- [ ] **Task 2: Scanning Practice:** Scan the article for specific numbers, names of researchers, and capitalized proper nouns in under 60 seconds.
- [ ] **Task 3: Collocations:** Learn 15 essential academic collocations (e.g., 'profound impact', 'substantial increase', 'viable alternative').
- [ ] **Task 4: Speaking Warmup:** Answer 4 standard Speaking Part 1 questions on 'Daily Routine & Hobbies' with natural intonation.
- [ ] **Task 5: Free Writing Drill:** Write 120 words describing your favorite hobby without pausing for grammar corrections.

---

### Day 5: Writing Task 1 Basics ✍️

> 💡 *Introduction to Academic Writing Task 1: Understanding graph elements, trends, and the 4-part report structure.*

- [ ] **Task 1: Task 1 Anatomy:** Learn the 4-paragraph structure: Introduction (Paraphrase), Overview (2 key trends), Body 1, Body 2.
- [ ] **Task 2: Trend Vocabulary:** Memorize verbs and adverbs for movement: soared, plummeted, fluctuated wildly, stabilized, plateaued.
- [ ] **Task 3: Paraphrasing Drill:** Take 3 sample Task 1 prompts and write 3 distinct paraphrased introductions for each.
- [ ] **Task 4: Listening Drill:** Listen to Section 1 audio from Cambridge 14 Test 1, taking notes on address spellings and numerical details.
- [ ] **Task 5: Typing Speed:** Type 250 words of sample Task 1 band 9 model answers to absorb academic sentence rhythms.

---

### Day 6: Grammar Core (Tenses & Voice) ⚙️

> 💡 *Eliminate tense confusion and master passive voice for scientific and procedural IELTS tasks.*

- [ ] **Task 1: Tense Contrast:** Study the difference between Past Simple vs Present Perfect vs Past Perfect in IELTS writing contexts.
- [ ] **Task 2: Passive Transformation:** Convert 10 active sentences describing manufacturing stages into formal passive voice constructions.
- [ ] **Task 3: Academic Vocab:** Learn 20 words related to the Environment & Climate (e.g., degradation, renewable, emission, biodiversity).
- [ ] **Task 4: Speaking Part 1:** Answer 4 questions on 'Weather & Seasons' using past and present perfect structures correctly.
- [ ] **Task 5: Error Correction:** Proofread and correct 10 common tense errors in sample IELTS student essays.

---

### Day 7: Weekly Review & Rest ☕

> 💡 *Consolidate your week 1 gains, audit your notebook, test your recall, and rest your mind.*

- [ ] **Task 1: Vocabulary Self-Test:** Review all 100+ words learned in Days 1–6 without looking at definitions; re-test weak words.
- [ ] **Task 2: Grammar Log:** Summarize your 3 most frequent grammar mistakes into your personal 'Forbidden Errors' checklist.
- [ ] **Task 3: Casual Immersion:** Watch 30 minutes of an English documentary (David Attenborough / BBC Earth) in a relaxed state.
- [ ] **Task 4: Rest & Plan:** Organize your study desk, organize week 2 materials, and take a long relaxing walk.

---

### Day 8: Advanced Vocabulary & Complex Sentences 🧠

> 💡 *Moving beyond simple sentences: compound-complex structures, relative clauses, and cohesive devices.*

- [ ] **Task 1: Complex Sentence Mastery:** Construct 5 sentences each using conditional clauses ('Unless...', 'Provided that...'), concessions ('Although...', 'Whereas...'), and relative pronouns.
- [ ] **Task 2: Topic Vocab (Education):** Learn 20 high-frequency terms related to Education & Pedagogy (curriculum, vocational, tertiary, pedagogical, rote learning).
- [ ] **Task 3: Listening Section 1 & 2:** Complete Section 1 & 2 of a Cambridge test, paying attention to spellings and singular/plural traps.
- [ ] **Task 4: Loud Reading:** Read 2 academic passages aloud with emphasis on pause phrasing at commas and semicolons.
- [ ] **Task 5: Typing Practice:** Type 300 words of complex sentences from a grammar workbook.

---

### Day 9: Listening Map & Speaking Part 1 Warm-up 🗺️

> 💡 *Crack the map labelling and directional prompts in Listening and expand spoken answers.*

- [ ] **Task 1: Map Directional Vocab:** Master listening directional language: 'adjacent to', 'bend in the road', 'opposite', 'south-west corner', 'roundabout'.
- [ ] **Task 2: Listening Map Drill:** Complete 2 Cambridge listening map labelling tasks, rewinding to identify missed spatial cues.
- [ ] **Task 3: Speaking Part 1 Expansion:** Practice answering Part 1 questions using the 'Area-Reason-Example' extension technique.
- [ ] **Task 4: Reading Skim Drill:** Skim a 600-word scientific article in 4 minutes, highlighting topic sentences.
- [ ] **Task 5: Typing Speed:** Type 20 lines of map directions and spatial prepositions.

---

### Day 10: Reading Comprehension & True/False/Not Given 🕵️‍♂️

> 💡 *Demystify the most misunderstood question type in IELTS Reading: True, False, and Not Given.*

- [ ] **Task 1: T/F/NG Logic:** Understand the golden rule: True = identical meaning; False = directly contradicts; Not Given = information missing or cannot be confirmed.
- [ ] **Task 2: Cambridge T/F/NG Drill:** Solve 10 T/F/NG questions from Cambridge 13/14 Passage 1, highlighting the exact evidence lines in the text.
- [ ] **Task 3: Qualifying Words Trap:** Identify qualifying words that flip T/F/NG answers: 'always', 'rarely', 'predominantly', 'alleged', 'unlikely'.
- [ ] **Task 4: Vocabulary:** Learn 15 synonyms for common IELTS keywords (e.g., discover/unearth, decline/deteriorate, crucial/imperative).
- [ ] **Task 5: Speaking Cue Card:** Prepare 1-minute notes for 'Describe a book or article you recently read' and record a 2-minute speech.

---

### Day 11: Writing Task 2 (Essay Structure & Outlining) 📝

> 💡 *Understand Band Descriptors for Task Achievement and Coherence. Master the standard 4-paragraph outline.*

- [ ] **Task 1: 4-Paragraph Formula:** Learn how to construct: Paragraph 1 (Background + Paraphrase + Clear Thesis), Body 1 (Idea + Evidence + Effect), Body 2 (Idea + Evidence + Effect), Paragraph 2 (Concluding Summary).
- [ ] **Task 2: Thesis Statement Drill:** Write 4 thesis statements for different prompt types: Agree/Disagree, Both Views, Causes/Solutions, Direct Question.
- [ ] **Task 3: Listening Section 2:** Solve Section 2 of a Cambridge test, focusing on speaker attitudes and distractors.
- [ ] **Task 4: Vocab Bank (Technology):** Learn 20 words for Technology & Automation (artificial intelligence, obsolete, ubiquity, algorithmic, telecommuting).
- [ ] **Task 5: Typing Essay Outlines:** Type 3 complete essay outlines (bullet points for Body 1 and Body 2) in 25 minutes.

---

### Day 12: Listening Dictation & Connected Speech 🗣️

> 💡 *Overcome native speaker speed, elision, and linked sounds that obscure answers.*

- [ ] **Task 1: Connected Speech Rules:** Study how native speakers link sounds (linking 'r', intrusive 'w', consonant-to-vowel linking).
- [ ] **Task 2: 1-Minute Dictation:** Listen to a 1-minute BBC audio snippet and write down every single word verbatim; compare with transcript.
- [ ] **Task 3: Reading Passage 1 Practice:** Solve an entire Cambridge Passage 1 (13 questions) under a 18-minute timer.
- [ ] **Task 4: Speaking Part 1 Drills:** Answer 5 questions on 'Music & Art' focusing on smooth sentence-stress patterns.
- [ ] **Task 5: Typing Vocab:** Type all newly encountered listening words and their phonetic pronunciations for 20 minutes.

---

### Day 13: Speaking Part 2 Strategy & 1-Minute Note-Taking 💡

> 💡 *Turn the 1-minute preparation time into an ironclad roadmap for unbroken 2-minute speaking.*

- [ ] **Task 1: Mind-Mapping Framework:** Learn the Who / When / Where / Why / How framework for organizing cue card bullet points during prep.
- [ ] **Task 2: Cue Card 1 Drill:** Topic: 'Describe a memorable journey you took.' Take 1 min notes, speak for 2 mins, record and listen.
- [ ] **Task 3: Cue Card 2 Drill:** Topic: 'Describe an older person you admire.' Take 1 min notes, speak for 2 mins, check if you hit all bullets.
- [ ] **Task 4: Writing Task 1 Overview:** Write 3 distinct 'Overview' paragraphs for line charts and bar charts without citing specific numbers.
- [ ] **Task 5: Vocabulary:** Learn 15 expressive adjectives for travel and character (scenic, arduous, hospitable, charismatic, resilient).

---

### Day 14: Reading Matching Headings & Passive Voice 🧩

> 💡 *Master the most time-consuming reading question type: Matching Headings to paragraphs.*

- [ ] **Task 1: Headings Strategy:** Learn to read headings first, underline keywords, and look for topic sentences and concluding syntheses.
- [ ] **Task 2: Matching Headings Drill:** Solve a Cambridge Matching Headings passage (6–8 paragraphs), analyzing why distractors are wrong.
- [ ] **Task 3: Listening Section 3 Intro:** Listen to a university tutorial conversation, noting tone of disagreement or concession.
- [ ] **Task 4: Grammar Practice:** Rewrite 10 sentences using inversion for emphasis ('Not only was the project costly, but it also...').
- [ ] **Task 5: Typing Speed Run:** Type 250 words of headings and summary notes in under 15 minutes.

---

### Day 15: Phase 1 Review & Diagnostic Checkpoint 🏆

> 💡 *Celebrate completing the first two weeks! Audit your vocabulary, review all mistake logs, and run a baseline check.*

- [ ] **Task 1: Vocabulary Mega-Quiz:** Test yourself on 150 vocabulary words learned across Days 1–14; flag words needing retention.
